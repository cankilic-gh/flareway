## Trademark and originality statement

The FT-172 is an original, logo-free high-wing trainer. It is inspired only by the broad aircraft category and published dimensional envelope of common four-seat trainers. It is not an official Cessna or Skyhawk product, is not endorsed by Textron Aviation, and contains no Cessna, Skyhawk, Textron, Garmin, Lycoming, real registration, badge, copied livery, manufacturer drawing, marketplace asset, scan, or AI-generated mesh.

The airfield is fictional. Runway markings and PAPI concepts follow public FAA descriptions, but no real airport layout is reproduced.
